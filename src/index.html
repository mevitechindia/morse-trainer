Exam mode

Morse test practice based on the Indian amateur radio exam: two tests, each 5 minutes at 8 WPM. To pass a test you need at least 1 continuous minute without any error. Hints are off during a test. Always check the current WPC rules for the exact exam format.

Receiving test

The app sends code at 8 WPM. Type what you hear as you go. Your score is the longest stretch you copied without a single mistake. Each test opens with an unscored VVV so your audio is warmed up; the clock starts after it.

Sending test

Key the text shown at 8 WPM with your . The clock starts on your first key-down. Wrong characters and error signals (8 dits or Backspace) count as errors. Your error-free minute must be sent at 8 WPM or faster, measured the standard (PARIS) way.

Plays an inaudible signal (about −80 dBFS) so Bluetooth, USB and power-saving audio devices don't fall asleep and clip the start of the code. Turn it off if you'd rather your audio device can sleep, e.g. to save headphone battery.
